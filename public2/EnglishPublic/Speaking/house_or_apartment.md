### 1. What kind of housing do you live in?
I live in a small apartment in the outskirts of Hanoi. It’s not very big, but it’s comfortable and convenient for my daily routine.

### 2. Do you live in a house or a flat?
I live in a flat. It’s part of a modern building with several floors and basic facilities like a parking area and a small garden.

### 3. Do you live alone or with your family?
I live with my family. We all share the same apartment, which makes the place feel warm and lively.

### 4. Could you please describe the place you live in?
My apartment has two bedrooms, a living room, a kitchen, and a small balcony. It’s simple but well-organized, and we get plenty of natural light during the day.

### 5. What did you like most about the house or apartment where you grew up?
I liked the sense of comfort the most. The neighborhood was peaceful, and my childhood home always felt safe and cozy.

### 6. Which is your favourite room in your home?
My favourite room is my bedroom because it’s the quietest place. It’s where I relax, study, and sometimes just enjoy music.

### 7. Is there anything which needs to be improved in your apartment?
Yes, the soundproofing could definitely be better. Sometimes I can hear noise from the hallway or neighbours, which is a bit annoying.

### 8. What can you see when you look out the window of your room?
From my window, I can see a few trees, some small shops, and a busy street. It’s not the most scenic view, but it’s quite lively.
