### 1. Which town or city do you live now?
I live in Hanoi, the capital city of Vietnam. It's a busy and lively place with a mix of modern buildings and traditional culture.

### 2. Are there any things you don't like about your area? (What are they?)
Yes, the traffic can be quite stressful, especially during rush hours. Sometimes the noise and pollution make the area less comfortable.

### 3. Do you think you will continue to live there for a long time? (Why/why not?)
I'm not completely sure. I like my area, but in the future I might move somewhere quieter or closer to nature. It depends on my job.

### 4. What are some changes in the area recently?
Recently, more cafes and small shops have opened. The roads have also been improved, which makes it easier to travel around.

### 5. Do you live in a house or an apartment?
I live in an apartment. It's not very large, but it's comfortable and convenient.

### 6. What is your favorite room in your home? Why?
My favorite room is my bedroom because it's the most peaceful place in the house. I can relax, listen to music, and forget about stress.

### 7. What things make your home pleasant to live in? Why?
The natural light and the simple decoration make my home pleasant. It feels cozy and welcoming, which helps me stay relaxed after a long day.

### 8. Are the people in your neighborhoods nice and friendly?
Yes, most of them are friendly. They often greet each other and sometimes help out when someone needs something.

### 9. Do you know any of your neighbors?
Yes, I know a few of them. We're not very close, but we say hello and chat sometimes.

### 10. Is the place where you live quiet or noisy?
It's a bit noisy during the day because of traffic, but it becomes much quieter at night.
