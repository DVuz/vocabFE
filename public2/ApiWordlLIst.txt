### 1. A popular place for sports (e.g. stadium)
**Part 2:** Where it is • When you went there • What you did there • How you felt about this place.
**Part 3:**
1. Do young people like to do sports?
2. Why do young people do less sport than their parents?

### 3 + 9. A time you received money on your birthday
**Part 2:** When it happened • Who gave you money • Why he/she gave you money • How you felt about it. *(Đề 9 thêm: What you did with it.)*
**Part 3:**
1. Why do people rarely use cash now?
2. Do you agree that nowadays people value money the most?
3. What do young people spend money on?
4. Why do young people tend to waste money?
5. Do people in your country like to save money?
6. What is the best way of saving money?
7. Why is it important for people to save some of their money?
8. Should children be taught how to manage money?
9. Do you think girls are better at saving money than boys?

✍️ **Writing:**
- *Children should be taught how to manage money at school. To what extent do you agree or disagree?*
- *(Problem–Solution)* *Many young people spend money carelessly. What are the causes and solutions?*
- *(Discuss both views)* *Some say money is the most important thing in life; others disagree.*
